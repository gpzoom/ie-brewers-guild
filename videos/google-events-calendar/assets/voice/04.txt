Next, select the new calendar's name and open "Settings and sharing". Scroll down to "Access permissions for events" and check: "Make available to public". If a warning window pops up, just hit "OK". After you click OK, the dropdown selector becomes clickable. Click the selector to change it from "See only free busy" to "See all event details". This one is important. If you don't choose to see all the details then nothing will show up on your profile or the homepage.
