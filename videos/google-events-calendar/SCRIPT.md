# Narration script — "Your events calendar, Google"

Export **one audio file per scene**, named `01` to `11` (MP3 or WAV).

## 01 — The promise
Your events can show up on the Guild homepage. And it happens on its own. Here's how to set it up with Google Calendar.

## 02 — Three steps
It takes three steps. Make an events calendar. Tag your events. Then paste one link.

## 03 — A calendar just for events
Start with a new calendar just for your events. Anyone with its link can see everything on it. So keep it separate from your own calendar. In Google Calendar press the plus next to Other calendars. Choose Create new calendar. Name it and press Create calendar.

## 04 — Make it public
Now point at the new calendar's name and open Settings and sharing. Under Access permissions for events tick Make available to public.

## 05 — See all event details
Right next to it is a menu. Change See only free busy to See all event details. This one matters. Without it Google hides your event names and nothing comes through.

## 06 — Copy the iCal link
Now scroll down to Integrate calendar. Copy the Public address in iCal format.

## 07 — Tag your events
Step two. Add your events to this calendar. Type hashtag guild in each event's title or description. Only tagged events come through.

## 08 — Paste the link and refresh
Step three. Sign in to the Member Portal and open Events. Press Add calendar. Paste your link and type the same tag. Then press Refresh now.

## 09 — On your profile
Your events show up on your profile right away. With the time and the place and your description.

## 10 — On the Guild homepage
Events in the next two weeks also appear on the Guild homepage. Each one gets its own calendar page with your logo and your color.

## 11 — Keep it going
From now on just add events to that calendar. The site checks for changes on its own. Google can take a few hours to update so give it a little time. Need help? Press Help in the Member Portal.
