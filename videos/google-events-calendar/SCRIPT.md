## 01 — The promise
Want your events to show up on the Guild homepage? Here's how to set it up with Google Calendar and have it run on autopilot.

## 02 — Three steps
It takes three steps. Create a dedicated events calendar. Create your events and tag them. Then paste one link in your Profile page. Do this setup on a computer because Google's phone app doesn't show the link you'll need.

## 03 — A calendar just for events
Step one starts with a new calendar just for your events. Just so you know, anyone with its link can see everything on it, so keep it separate from your own personal calendar. Open Google Calendar and press the plus next to "Other" calendars. Choose Create new calendar. Name it and press "Create calendar".

## 04 — Make it public and show all details
Next, select the new calendar's name and open "Settings and sharing". Scroll down to "Access permissions for events" and check: "Make available to public". If a warning window pops up, just hit "OK". After you click OK, the dropdown selector becomes clickable. Click the selector to change it from "See only free busy" to "See all event details". This one is important. If you don't choose to see all the details then nothing will show up on your profile or the homepage.

## 05 — Copy the iCal link
Now scroll down to "Integrate calendar". Copy the "Public address in iCal format". This will be used in Step three, so you may want to paste it somewhere you can grab it when you get there.

## 06 — Tag your events
Step two. Add your events to this new calendar. Type hashtag guild in each event's title or description. Don't forget this step because only tagged events will show up on your profile and the homepage.

## 07 — Paste the link and refresh
Step three. Grab the link you copied at the end of step one.  Sign in to your Member Portal and open Events. Press Add calendar. Paste your link and type the same tag you used in your tagged events (hashtag guild). Then press Refresh now.

## 08 — On your profile
Your events show up on your profile right away. With the time and the place and your description.

## 09 — On the Guild homepage
Events that are scheduled within the next two weeks also appear on the Guild homepage. Each one gets its own calendar page with your logo and your theme color.

## 10 — Keep it going
From now on just add events to your dedicated events calendar, on your phone or your computer. The site checks for changes on its own. Google can take a few hours to update so give it a little time. Need help? Press Help in the Member Portal.
