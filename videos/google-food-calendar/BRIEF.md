# Brief — "Your food vendor calendar, Google"

- **Who it's for:** taproom owners (producers). It plays from a "Watch how to connect a Google Calendar for FOOD VENDORS" link on the portal's **Food** page, the wizard's Food step and `/admin/food`.
- **What it teaches:** make a separate Google calendar for visiting food vendors (trucks and pop-ups alike, called "food vendors"), make it public with all event details, add each visit tagged #food (vendor name in the title, menu link in the description), paste the iCal link on the Food page, then Refresh now.
- **What they see at the end:** "Food this week" on their profile: today first, then the next six days with one tap. Days without a vendor say "Bring your own food", or "Closed" when the posted hours say so. Food is **not** shown on the Guild homepage.
- **Look and voice:** same as `../google-events-calendar/` (frame.md, fonts, "My Voice v3" on ElevenLabs, captions from the narration).
- **Reuse:** scenes 3–5 reuse the events video's Google Calendar scenes, with the new calendar named "Bob's Brewery Food". Scene 5 reuses its narration audio too.
- **Screens needed:** Bob's Brewery's Food page with a connected food calendar, and its profile's "Food this week" showing a day with vendors, a "Bring your own food" day and a "Closed" day.
