# Brief — "Your food vendor calendar, Google"

- **Who it's for:** taproom owners (producers). It plays from a "Watch how to connect a Google Calendar for FOOD VENDORS" link on the portal's **Food** page, the wizard's Food step and `/admin/food`.
- **What it teaches (owner, 2026-09-30): one calendar, two tags.** Vendor visits go on the **same Google calendar as their events**, tagged **#food** (vendor name in the title, menu link in the description). Then the **same link** is pasted on the Food page with the tag #food, and Refresh now is pressed. A separate food calendar is still allowed; the Food page's hint mentions it, the video doesn't.
- **Assumes** the events calendar is already connected. It points to the events video if not.
- **What they see at the end:** "Food for the next week" on their profile: all seven days, today first (under the photos on desktop). Days without a vendor say "Bring your own food", or "Closed" when the posted hours say so. Food is **not** shown on the Guild homepage.
- **Look and voice:** same as `../google-events-calendar/` (frame.md, fonts, "My Voice v3" on ElevenLabs, captions from the narration). No scenes are reused from it: this video skips creating and sharing a calendar.
- **Screens needed (Bob's Brewery):**
  - its existing events calendar with 2–3 vendor visits this week tagged #food, and at least one empty day;
  - the same link connected on its Food page with #food;
  - at least one closed day in its hours;
  - then its Food page and its profile's "Food for the next week" (a vendor day, a "Bring your own food" day, a "Closed" day).
