# Narration script — "Sending email from your guild address"

For recording with your ElevenLabs voice clone. One block per scene.
Edit anything, then rebuild. Keep the `## NN — Title` lines as they are, since the build reads them.
Spoken spellings ("I S C", "dot org", "smtp dot resend dot com") are turned back into
written addresses in the captions by build/build.py (CAPTION_FIXES).

## 01 — Welcome
Welcome. This video shows you how to send email from your guild address, like president at I S C brewers guild dot org, right from the guild Gmail account. Mail sent to your guild address already arrives in that inbox. Today we add the sending side. It takes about five minutes, and you only do it once.

## 02 — Before you start
You'll need three things. A computer, because the Gmail phone app can't do this step. The guild Gmail account, signed in. And the sending password the webmaster gave you, by phone or in person. Keep that password private, and never email it.

## 03 — Open settings
Step one. Open Gmail and check the picture at the top right. It must be the guild account, not your personal one. Click the gear icon, then See all settings. Open the Accounts and Import tab. Find Send mail as, and click Add another email address.

## 04 — Name and address
Step two. A small window opens. If it doesn't, your browser blocked the pop-up, so allow pop-ups for Gmail and click again. Type the name people will see, like I S C Brewers Guild President, and your guild address. Leave Treat as an alias checked, and click Next Step.

## 05 — Server settings
Step three, the server settings. Type them exactly. The SMTP server is smtp dot resend dot com. Pick port 465. The username is just the word resend. For the password, paste the sending password from the webmaster. Choose Secured connection using SSL, then click Add Account.

## 06 — Confirm
Step four. Gmail sends a confirmation email to your guild address, and it arrives right back in this same inbox. Open it and click the link. Nothing after a couple of minutes? Check your Spam folder.

## 07 — Reply setting
One last setting. Back on Accounts and Import, find When replying to a message, and choose Reply from the same address the message was sent to. Now when someone writes to your guild address, your reply goes out from it too.

## 08 — Try it
Let's test it. Click Compose, click the From line, and pick your guild address. Send a test to your personal email. There it is, from the guild address. You're all set, and you never need to do this again.

## 09 — Ground rules
A few ground rules. Check the From line before every send. No mass mailings. The guild can send one hundred emails a day in total, shared with the website. Use it for guild business only, since the inbox passes to the next officer. And never share either password by email or text.

## 10 — Need help
That's it. The written guide has every setting and a troubleshooting list. If you get stuck, send an email to webmaster, at I S C Brewers Guild dot org. Thanks for serving the guild.
