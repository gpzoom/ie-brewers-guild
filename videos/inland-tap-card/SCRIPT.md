# Narration script — "The Inland Tap Card: one card, two jobs"

Internal video for current Guild members. Goal: get members excited about the Tap Card and
recruiting other businesses into the Guild. Voiced with the owner's ElevenLabs clone
(`build/eleven_tts.py`), one audio file per `## NN — Title` block. Commission stays general (no rate, no dollar math).

## 01 — What if
What if your Guild membership paid for itself? And what if a customer loyalty program came with it, free? That's the Inland Tap Card. Let me show you how it works.

## 02 — One card, two jobs
The Tap Card lives on your customer's phone. There's no app to download and no plastic to lose. And it does two jobs. It's a Beer Trail pass, and it's a loyalty card at every Guild member.

## 03 — Job one: the Beer Trail
Job one is the Beer Trail. Customers buy a pass for the year. At every stop on the trail, their first beer is just a nickel, and they earn Guild rewards as they hit milestones. It gives people a reason to walk into taprooms they've never visited, including yours.

## 04 — You earn on every pass
Here's the part I'm most excited about. When a customer joins the Beer Trail at your counter, that sale is credited to you, and you earn a share of every pass you sell. Sell enough of them and your commission can offset your dues, or even cover them completely. You'll see your passes and your commission, month by month, right on your Tap Card page.

## 05 — Job two: free loyalty
Job two is loyalty. Every Tap Card is also a loyalty card, at every Guild member, not just breweries. You choose the rewards. A free pretzel on visit three. Two dollars off every fifth visit. Whatever fits your business. And it costs you nothing. Visits never reset, so when the trail year ends, the card keeps right on working.

## 06 — Your own email list
When customers sign up at your counter, they can check a box to hear from you. Your Tap Card page shows how many have said yes, so you can watch your list of regulars grow.

## 07 — At the counter
At the counter, it takes seconds. The customer scans your counter plate. They check in, and collect any rewards they've qualified for. Your staff member holds up the staff QR code, the customer scans it, and they show you a big green screen. That's your signal to pour.

## 08 — Getting set up
Getting set up is easy. Open Tap Card in your Member Portal. Set a four-digit staff code, print your counter plate and your staff QR code, and add your rewards.

## 09 — Grow the Guild
Now here's where YOU come in. Every business that joins the Guild makes the Tap Card worth more to customers and to every one of us. Every new producer is another stop on the trail. Every new member is another place the card works. So think about that brewery down the road, or the food truck out back. Tell them about the Tap Card, and send them our way. Let's grow this together.
