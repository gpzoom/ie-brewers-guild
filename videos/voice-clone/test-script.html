Voice Clone Mic Test

About 1 minute · record this before the full sessions so the setup can be checked

How to record the test

  • In Audacity, press Record, then stay silent for 5 seconds. Don't move, and hold the paper still.
  • Read the three paragraphs below at your normal, friendly pace.
  • Stay silent for 2 more seconds, then press Stop.
  • File → Export Audio → WAV, 16-bit, named test.wav, saved in videos oice-clone\.

Test paragraphs

Paragraphs 1–3 of the main script
1

Welcome to the Guild. We're glad you're here. Over the next few minutes, I'll show you how to set up your profile, step by step. There's nothing to memorize, and nothing you can break. If you get stuck, you can always come back to this video and pick up where you left off.

2

Your profile is the page people see when they look you up on the Guild website. It's where they find your hours, your address, your beers, and what's coming up at your place. A complete profile helps people decide to visit. An empty one sends them somewhere else.

3

Let's start by signing in. Go to the Guild website and press Sign in, at the top right of the page. Type your email address and your password, and press Continue. If you've forgotten your password, press Forgot password, and we'll send you a link to make a new one. That's it. You're in.
