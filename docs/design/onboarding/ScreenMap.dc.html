What exists today

The portal screens are already built (F, G, I, K, M, R, H). New work is the wizard chrome, Welcome, Review, the "Finish your profile" card and the You're live screen.

Decided: edits save to a draft

Typing saves to a draft, never the live page. Preview shows the draft. Publish changes copies it live. While they differ the top bar shows "Unpublished changes" and Discard changes, which (after a confirm) resets the draft to what is live.
