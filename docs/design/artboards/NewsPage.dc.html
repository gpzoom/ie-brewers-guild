Inland Southern California Brewers Shine at the 2026 Great American Beer Festival

Breweries from our region brought home two gold medals and a bronze from the Great American Beer Festival in Denver, and four more beers reached the final table.

The Great American Beer Festival competition is one of the toughest in the country, with dozens to hundreds of entries in every category. This year, brewers from across Inland Southern California earned three medals and four final-table spots. Congratulations to everyone who entered, and to these winners and finalists.

Medals

  • Gold
    No Fly Zone 8 Bit Brewing Co. · Murrieta New Zealand-Style India Pale Ale (89 entries)
  • Gold
    Happy Days Claremont Craft Ales · Claremont Strong Red Ale (32 entries)
  • Bronze
    Pepper and Peaches Claremont Craft Ales · Claremont Specialty India Pale Ale (65 entries)

Final table

  • Finalist
    Festbier Claremont Craft Ales · Claremont Dortmunder or German-Style Festbier (161 entries)
  • Finalist
    Uncle Fester Evans Brewing Co. · Corona Dortmunder or German-Style Festbier (161 entries)
  • Finalist
    My Favorite Marzen Mars Brewing Co. · Rancho Cucamonga German-Style Maerzen (184 entries)
  • Finalist
    Munich Lager Bavarian Style-Maerzen Ritual Brewing Co. · Redlands German-Style Maerzen (184 entries)
