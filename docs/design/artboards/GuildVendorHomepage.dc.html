On the homepage: once, as the taproom’s event
A Guild member’s stop at a taproom counts as the taproom’s event (owner, 6 Oct 2026). The carousel shows one card, in Mars’s colors, “with” the member — not a second card for the truck.
If Mars hides the stop, the card goes back to being the truck’s own event card, as it is today. A stop anywhere else is the member’s own card, unchanged.
