Inland Scan Pass

Your first beer for a nickel.

One pass for every independent taproom on the trail. Discover a brewery you’ve never been to.

  1. 1

    Get your pass

    Scan the QR code at any stop’s counter to get an Inland Scan Pass on your phone and join the Beer Trail.

  2. 2

    Scan in at every new taproom

    Scan the counter plate, then the staff QR code. Your visit counts, and you get that stop’s offer once a year.

  3. 3

    Earn Guild rewards

    Visit more stops to earn rewards from the Guild. Visits count — not how much you drink.
