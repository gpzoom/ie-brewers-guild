Inland Southern California Brewers Guild

Your member profile:
a quick guide

Every Guild member has a page on the Guild's member directory at iscbrewersguild.org. This guide lists what to gather before you start, and walks you through each step of the setup.

How to sign in

  1. Go to iscbrewersguild.org and scroll to the bottom of the page.
  2. Click Member Portal.
  3. Enter the email address the Guild has for your business. There's no password.
  4. Open the email we send you and click the sign-in link. Use the same phone or computer.
  5. The setup wizard opens and walks you through each step.

Good to know

  • About 10 minutes.Longer if you're choosing photos.
  • Only the first three steps are required.Everything after "The basics" can be skipped and finished later.
  • Save & exit any time.Your work is saved as you type. Sign in again to pick up where you left off.
  • Nothing goes live until you publish.Preview your page first, then publish when it looks right.

Which type of member are you?

The Guild has already set your type. You'll confirm it in step 2. It decides which parts of your page appear, so some steps in this guide apply only to one type. Look for these tags:

Producer

Producer with a taproom

Brewery, meadery, cidery or distillery the public can visit. Your page shows weekly hours, "Open now" and directions.

Mobile

Mobile member

Entertainment, food truck or pop-up food vendor. Your page shows where you'll be next instead of weekly hours.

Allied

Allied Member

Supply house, ingredients, equipment or services. Your page shows business hours, a trade contact and your Guild member discount.

Is the wrong type showing? Pick the right one in step 2 by choosing That's not right. After that, your type is locked. To change it later, use Request a type change on Basics & hours and the Guild will take care of it.

After you're live

Signing in to Member Portal now opens your profile editor. A Finish your profile card lists anything still empty. Edits save to a draft. Your page only changes when you click Publish changes. Event changes show right away.

Want help keeping it up to date?

The owner can invite others under People: a Full editor can edit everything, and a Photos & events editor can edit just those two sections. Each person gets their own sign-in email.

Questions? Reach the Guild through the contact form at iscbrewersguild.org or email iscbrewersguild@gmail.com.
